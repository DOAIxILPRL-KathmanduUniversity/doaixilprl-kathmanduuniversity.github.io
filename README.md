# DoAI × ILPRL | Kathmandu University NLP Internship Index

Official website and project index for the joint Natural Language Processing (NLP) capstone internship initiatives between the **Department of Artificial Intelligence (DoAI)** and the **Information and Language Processing Research Lab (ILPRL)** at Kathmandu University.

## Academic Leadership & Mentorship
- **Mr. Sunil Regmi** — Lecturer & Bachelor Program Coordinator, Department of Artificial Intelligence (DoAI), Kathmandu University
- **Prof. Dr. Bal Krishna Bal** — Professor of Computer Engineering & Dean, School of Engineering, Kathmandu University; Founding Lead, ILPRL

## Research Thrust
Strictly focused on Natural Language Processing (NLP) for Nepali and languages of Nepal:
- Low-Resource Corpus Curation & Provenance
- Standardized Benchmarking & Model Evaluation (BalEval)
- Annotation Frameworks & Task Hubs (Nepali Data Studio)
- Speech (ASR), Devanagari OCR, and Document Intelligence
